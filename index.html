Welcome Message

Under the presidency of Prof. Dr. Abdo El-Sewisy

Dear Esteemed Professors, Distinguished Doctors, Honored Guests, and Valued Participants,

On behalf of the Organizing Committee, and under the distinguished presidency of Prof. Dr. Abdo El-Sewisy, it is our great honor and pleasure to welcome you to MISSUE 2027, taking place from 27 to 29 January 2027 in the historic and enchanting city of Aswan, Egypt.

This scientific gathering brings together leading professors, renowned experts, and dedicated healthcare professionals from across the region and beyond, united by a shared commitment to advancing medical knowledge and improving patient care. Over three days, the program will offer a rich blend of scientific sessions, expert-led discussions, live demonstrations, and interactive workshops, all designed to reflect the latest developments and best practices in our field.

We sincerely hope that your participation will be both rewarding and inspiring, offering valuable opportunities to exchange experiences, explore new perspectives, build lasting professional connections, and foster collaborations that extend well beyond the conference halls.

Your active participation, thoughtful contributions, and valuable insights are essential to the success of this event and to creating an atmosphere of academic excellence and mutual respect. We are deeply grateful for the time and effort each of you has dedicated to being with us.

We also invite you to take a moment to enjoy the warmth of Egyptian hospitality and the timeless beauty of Aswan, with its golden Nile views, ancient heritage, and welcoming spirit.

We wish you an enjoyable stay and a fruitful, enriching, and memorable conference.

About MISSUE 2027

A three-day scientific gathering dedicated to advancing knowledge and clinical practice in Obstetrics and Gynecology.

Taking place in Aswan, Egypt, from 27–29 January 2027, MISSUE 2027 aims to provide an engaging educational experience through practical training, scientific competition, and high-quality academic discussions. By combining practical education with advanced scientific discussions, the conference creates an inspiring platform for knowledge exchange, professional collaboration, and continuous medical development.
